To use your own backend, create a Service named "otel-collector" in the
tentacular-observability namespace. Options:

  ExternalName (in-cluster collector):
    kubectl -n tentacular-observability create service externalname otel-collector \
      --external-name my-collector.monitoring.svc.cluster.local

  Manual Endpoints (external IP):
    See the Tentacular docs "Bring Your Own Observability" guide.
